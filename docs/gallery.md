# Mandir event gallery

The homepage section previously headed “A sanctuary of faith” is now a seven-photo Gallery. It retains the `#our-mandir` anchor for existing navigation.

Photographs: [SRMS Institutions' Deepdan Mahotsav archive, 4 November 2022](https://www.srms.ac.in/deepdan-mahotsav-at-srms-captivated-realm-of-devotion/). The files `gallery-deepdan-1.jpg` through `gallery-deepdan-7.jpg` correspond to `Deep-Ekadashi-1.jpg` through `Deep-Ekadashi-7.jpg` in the archive's `/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/` directory. The images were visually checked to show the SRMS CET Bareilly mandir and its grounds. The gallery labels them as 2022 archive photographs.

The slideshow advances every five seconds and repeats after photo seven, with an 800 ms fade and gentle zoom. Previous/next arrows, seven photo thumbnails and a pause/play control allow manual browsing. Hover and keyboard focus pause automatic rotation. Reduced-motion preferences disable automatic rotation and transitions.

The public sign-in page now uses a labelled temple dropdown instead of the “Wrong temple?” link. Selecting another temple clears entered credentials and errors and opens that temple's sign-in URL, without carrying a booking redirect from another temple.
